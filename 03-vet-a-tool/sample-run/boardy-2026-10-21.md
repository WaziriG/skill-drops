# Sample run: Boardy, October 21 2026

A real run of `/vet-a-tool`, kept as an example. The quotes come from Boardy's privacy policy (last updated June 26 2026) and terms (last updated September 28 2026), as read on October 21 2026. **Policies change. If you use Boardy, run the check yourself against today's pages:** https://www.boardy.ai/privacy-policy and https://www.boardy.ai/terms-and-conditions

The story behind this run: [One Link Deeper](https://wazirigaruba.com/one-link-deeper)

## The answers

- **Q1, the job:** "making meaningful introductions."
- **Q2, the data:** today, public details about my program. Later, possibly prospective clients, and possibly alumni from my business school. "It depends." Boardy had already asked twice for a list of 15 to 20 owners from my network, and I said no both times. Two clients were copied in as references, with their agreement.
  → **Tier 1.** It falls between tiers, and the movement is the flag: "it depends" is how a tool ends up holding more than you approved.
- **Q3, lanes:** D (several clients, each confidential).
- **Q4, non-negotiables:**
  1. A person goes in only after saying yes to the introduction. Never a list.
  2. No deal context: no client's money, problems or terms.
  3. No data from the alumni association's records.
  4. Before anything Tier 1: a training opt-out, a list of who it shares with, and a written breach commitment. If the answer is unknown, it counts as a No.
- **Q5, the vendor's pages:** the pages at /privacy and /terms were short marketing pages with none of the answers. The dated policies were one link deeper.

## What the policies said (verbatim, October 21 2026)

- "No training of AI models on proprietary business information without consent." This sits under the enterprise section. Elsewhere, conversations are used to "Improve our services," and "Communication records: Retained for 3 years for service improvement and support." No opt-out for conversations was found.
- Data is processed by "third-party AI services including OpenAI, Anthropic, and other AI technology providers." Meta is named for advertising and PostHog for website analytics. No full subprocessor list was found.
- "Boardy may use your contact information (email addresses and phone numbers) in hashed or encrypted formats to create custom audiences on social media platforms, including Meta."
- "The Boardy team has access to and monitors: User conversations with Boardy."
- "we will notify you without undue delay" (for high-risk breaches), and "24-hour notification commitment for affected businesses."
- "Your email address and/or phone number will only be shared with another user after both parties have explicitly opted in."

## The verdict

```
TOOL:            Boardy — meaningful introductions
DATA TIER:       Tier 1 — between public program details and client and alumni contact data
LANES:           D
NON-NEGOTIABLES: 1. Consenting people only, never a list  2. No deal context
                 3. No alumni records  4. Opt-out, named sharers, written breach terms before Tier 1
CHECKED:         Breach notice: written. Double opt-in: written.
                 Training opt-out for conversations: not found. Full list of who it shares with: not found.
DISQUALIFIERS:   Cannot name everyone it shares with → never touches Tier 1.
                 No conversation opt-out found in fifteen minutes → Tier 1 is a No.

VERDICT:         Permissible with a written limit
THE LIMIT:       "Boardy hears what I'm looking for and who I am. It never gets a list,
                  a client's business, or an alum's details. A person reaches Boardy
                  only by saying yes first."
STILL UNKNOWN:   Does the training line cover user conversations, and can it be switched off?
                 (privacy@boardy.ai) Full list of who it shares with. Where data is stored.
```

This is one operator's verdict for one job on one date. It is not a rating of Boardy, and your answer depends on your data and your lanes.
