# Sample run: a 500 GB MacBook Pro, Oct 24–25 2026

My own first run. The folder names are generalized, but the numbers are real.

## Before

- 500 GB disk, **11 GB free**. The Mac kept showing "low memory" warnings.
- Swap (the disk space macOS uses when memory fills up) was 13.4 of 14.3 GB used. The full disk was the real problem.
- Two 4 TB USB hard drives with about 1.3 TB free each. A third drive was ruled out because it was NTFS (read-only on a Mac) and failed its health check. A network drive at 94% full was also ruled out.

## The register

60 folders of 250 MB or more, 121 GB in total. I marked each one *keep*, *archive* or *pull back*. 27 folders (61 GB) were approved to move: old client folders, a reference library, a wedding video archive, and a screenshot archive.

## Copy

Every file went to both drives with a fingerprint check on each copy. 0 failures on either drive. It ran overnight at about 5 MB/s, slower than usual because another job was reading the same drives.

## Check and clear

Right before deleting, every file on the Mac was fingerprinted again and compared with both drive copies. Two folders were held back:

- One folder had a backslash in its name (`Receipts \ Invoices`), and the copy log had recorded it differently. I checked those 18 files by hand on both drives, and all of them matched.
- One folder held two Windows leftovers (`Thumbs.db`, `desktop.ini`) that are skipped on purpose.

Then I cleared 24 folders: **20,434 files, 64.1 GB**. Each one now holds a READ-ME naming both drive locations. Three folders I'd approved earlier were switched back to *keep*, so their drive copies became backups only.

## After

- Free space at first: **still 11 GB**. A Time Machine local snapshot was holding the deleted files.
- A few hours later the snapshot expired on its own: **71 GB free**.

## What it was worth

| What it replaces | Value |
|---|---:|
| Buying the next Mac with 1 TB instead of 2 TB | ~$400 |
| A 2 TB cloud plan for the same files, 3 years | ~$360 |
| The same 64 GB as built-in storage | ~$26 |
| Two drive copies | $0 (I already owned the drives) |

The 64 GB on its own is worth very little. The money is in the next computer: with this system in place, I can buy less storage and put the money into memory.
