# Sample run: a bakery owner after a call with a new wholesale lead

Every person and business here is invented. The notes this run used are in `sample-call-notes.txt` in this folder, so you can run the same thing yourself and compare.

**What Maya typed:**

```
/follow-up-email
```

Then she pasted the notes.

---

## 1. The promise list

Sender: Maya Torres. Recipient: Ben Whitfield, Harbor Lane Workspace.

| # | Who promised | What | Their words (or closest line in the notes) | Deadline said | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maya | Wholesale price sheet | "I said I'd send him our wholesale price sheet today" | Today | Ready? |
| 2 | Maya | Photos of the two gluten-free items | "I'd send photos of both" | None | Ready? |
| 3 | Maya | The bookstore event menu (soft offer) | "I could send you the menu we did for the bookstore event" | None | Ready? |
| 4 | Maya | A tasting date for the member committee | "I'd check our Thursday schedule with Luis ... and get back to him with a date" | None | Needs a date |
| 5 | Maya | Introduce Ben's cafe friend to her cousin Ana | "you should meet my cousin Ana ... I'd introduce them" | None | Check first |
| 6 | Ben | Delivery dock hours and who signs for deliveries | "Ben said he'd send me their delivery dock hours" | None | Theirs |

**Flags**
- Ben said the vendor situation is "between us for now." It stays out of the email.
- Ben said "around four hundred a week but don't hold me to it." That is a loose number. It is not repeated in the email.
- His decision deadline is **Nov 20**, so the tasting needs to land before then.

**Questions (one message):**
1. Items 1 to 3: links or attachments?
2. Item 4: what Thursday can Luis do?
3. Item 5: have you asked Ana yet?

**Maya's answers:** "Price sheet and photos are attachments. I don't have the bookstore menu handy, I'll find it tonight. Luis can do Thursday the 12th, 10am. Haven't asked Ana."

Maya
